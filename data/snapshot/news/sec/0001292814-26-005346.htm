TAX REPORT 3rd Quarter 2026 INTERNA \ Força de Trabalho TAX REPORT – 3rd Quarter 2026 This Tax Report details the cash outflows made by Petrobras for the payment of taxes and government take in the first nine months of 2026. The information follows the cash basis method and should be read along with the 2025 Tax Report, which presents the Company’s compliance aspects, tax risk management and tax policy, among other information aboutour role as one of the largest taxpayers inthe Brazilianeconomy. From January to September 2026, Petrobras contributed R$ 251.3 billion to public coffers, which corresponds to an amount 26% higher than the same period of 2025. From this total, R$ 168.0 billion corresponded to taxes directly paid by Petrobras, R$ 58.1 billion to government take revenues (PGOV), and R$25.2 billion to taxes withheld on behalf ofthird parties. Year-to-date through the third quarter of 2026, Petrobras paid R$ 97.6 billion in federal taxes and R$ 58.1 billion in government take, totaling R$ 155.7 billion allocated to the Federal Government, which transfers a portion of these resources to states and municipalities in accordance with the current legislation. This amount corresponds to approximately 6.6% of federal tax revenues during the period¹. Compared with the same period of 2025, federal tax payments increased by 35%, mainly due to the higher corporate income tax collections and the introduction of the Export Tax. At the state level, Petrobras contributed R$ 94.0 billion, equivalent to approximately 15.1% of total state tax revenues². Compared with the January–September 2025 period, this represents an increase of 13%, driven by highersales volumes and theincrease in the nominal ICMS rate leviedon products subject to the single-phase tax regime. Municipal taxes totaled approximately R$ 1.6 billion, representing a 6% increase compared with the same period of 2025. This growth was mainly attributable to the contracting of services for refining activities. Payments to municipalities are concentrated primarily in ISS withheld at source on behalf of third parties (ISS-ST), ISS directly paid by Petrobras, and Urban Property Tax (IPTU). ________________ 1-2 Source: Federal Revenue Service of Brazil (Receita Federal do Brasil) and the transparency portals of the states. R$ 155.7 billion In March 2026, Provisional Measure No. 1,340 introduced the Export Tax (IE) on exports of crude petroleum oils or oils obtained from bituminous minerals, as well as road diesel fuel, effective as of March 12, 2026. Following the expiration ofthe provisional measure on July 9, 2026, the taxation of crude oil exports was maintained through Gecex Resolutions Nos. 938, 957 and 977/2026 until December 31, 2026. The Export Tax on crude oil is generally levied at a 12% rate and at a 6% rate on exports to the European Union, pursuant to the Interim Trade Agreement between the European Union (EU) and the Southern Common Market (Mercosur). Through September 2026, Petrobras' Export Tax payments totaled R$ 6.9 billion.
