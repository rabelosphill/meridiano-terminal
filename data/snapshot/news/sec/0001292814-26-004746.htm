Responsible: Technical Executive Vice-Presidency and Executive Vice- President of Sustainability. Published in: 2026/09/21. Review by: 2031/09/21. - 1 de 3 - Administrative Policy 1. General Guidelines Vale believes that mining plays a fundamental role in providing resources that drive economic development and social well-being and recognizes the management and use of Mining Waste as an important challenge in the sector, given the scale of generation, as well as the operating conditions, the regulations applicable to the subject and the different forms of management and disposal of Waste and Tailings. In this context, Vale is committed to carrying out a differentiated management of Mining Waste, in accordance with the guidelines established in this Policy, which are directly related to the technologies adopted in the mine planning and ore processing processes. The guidelines below must be observed in order to reduce the generation and reuse of Waste and Tailings in Vale's operations, in order to support the transition to a Circular Economy, which seeks to increase efficiency in the use of materials, through the promotion of new technologies and use in other value chains. This transition towards a Circular Economy is integrated into the mining sector through Circular Mining, which is a strategic pillar for the Company. • Act with transparency, ethics, and responsibility: be transparent in the accountability of the actions taken and the commitments assumed, taking ownership of the respective consequences, acting with zeal, responsibility, and respect for the legislation. • Prioritize, in the phases of the mine's useful life, available reference practices and technologies, seeking the optimization of mineral research, mining and processing, the reduction of the generation of Tailings and Waste Waste, in addition to the use of materials already disposed of in geotechnical mining structures, considering the technical, economic and safety feasibility. • Encourage the development of new technologies focused on equipment and controls in ore processing processes, aiming to increase efficiency and reduce the generation of Tailings and Waste Waste. • Invest in Research, Development and Innovation ("RD&I"), seeking to promote partnerships for the insertion of Co-products in other production chains and create shared value with society. • Consider, from the design phase to the mine closure phase, the geochemical characterization processes of ore, waste and tailings, in order to subsidize control actions and reduce potential socio-environmental impacts. • To strategically manage the risks and opportunities associated with the topic, aiming to promote operational excellence, including eco-efficiency, the responsible generation and disposal of Mining Waste and the conservation of biodiversity. 2. Definitions Use of Tailings and Waste Waste: Use or processing of materials previously deposited in waste rock piles or dams/piles and/or tailings, aiming at the recovery of mineral substances of interest, use in other production processes, or direct application in other sectors. Customers: Any customer, including its intermediaries, of products or services of Vale or its Subsidiaries. Co-products: For the purposes of this Policy, materials of mineral origin generated in the production process that do not generate economically viable production of iron ore, but that have the potential to be used as raw material/input in other production processes or to be used directly as a product in other sectors, such as industrial, agricultural and construction sectors. Employees: For the purposes of this Policy, any employee of Vale and/or its Subsidiaries, permanent or temporary, interns, young apprentices and/or trainees Circular Economy2: It is the economic system of production that maintains the circular flow of resources and associates economic activity with the circular management of resources, through the addition, retention or recovery of their values, and which is based on the principles of non-generation of waste, circulation of products and materials and regeneration Waste: Material removed in mining, without value or with low economic value, and which is usually disposed of in piles before the processing process, not going through this stage. It is considered a product resulting from mining, owned by the miner and can be processed later, as well as reused in infrastructure works or other technically viable applications. Suppliers: Any supplier of goods or service provider, including consultants, agents, commercial representatives, brokers, intermediaries, among others. 2 Decree 12,082, of June 27, 2024: Establishes the National Circular Economy Strategy. - 3 of 4 - Circular Mining Policy POL-0040-G Rev.: 01 – 2026/09/21 DDE 078/2026 PUBLIC Circular Mining3: Practice that seeks to maximize the use of natural resources, with the objective of preventing the generation of waste and reusing materials of mineral origin, ferrous and non-ferrous, such as Waste and Tailings, while generating value and reducing socio-environmental impacts. Partners: Any entities (associations, institutions, organizations, etc.) with which Vale and/or its Subsidiaries enter into some type of commercial, technical, social, institutional partnership, among others, which are not configured as a Customer or Supplier. Stakeholders: Communities, press, investors, Employees, public agencies, NGOs and academia, Customers and Suppliers. Mining Tailings: For the purposes of this Policy, it is the material discarded during and/or after the mineral processing process, composed of fine particles of rocks and minerals, generated in the form of aqueous pulp or dry fractions.
