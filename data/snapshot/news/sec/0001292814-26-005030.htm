Diesel sales volume grew 14.4%, driven by the planting of the summer crop and increased industrial activity, which traditionally boost fuel consumption in the third quarter. Jet fuel sales grew 9.9%, reflecting higher demand during the July school holidays. The increase was also supported by the lower 2Q26 comparison base, when higher international prices led airlines to step up fleet optimization. LPG sales increased 1.8%, supported by higher activity in the manufacturing industry, lower average temperatures in the South and Southeast regions — the country’s main consumer centers — and more business days in the period. In the opposite direction, gasoline sales decreased 0.7%, influenced by the increase in the anhydrous ethanol content of the blend, from 30% to 32%, effective since August 1, 2026. Naphtha sales, in turn, fell 10.5% compared with 2Q26, mainly due to lower sales to the petrochemical complex in Rio Grande do Sul. Fuel oil sales also declined, by 5.3% compared with the previous quarter, mainly due to lower sales to the industrial segment in the South and Southeast regions. By contrast, sales in the maritime segment grew, driven by higher demand for vessel bunkering at Brazilian ports amid the conflict in the Middle East.
