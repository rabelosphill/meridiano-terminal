Consolidated Synthetic Map

  

 

Extraordinary General Meeting on July 22, 2026

 

Rio de Janeiro, July 21, 2026 – Pursuant to CVM Resolution No. 81/2021, as amended, Vale S.A. (“Vale” or the “Company”) hereby discloses the Synthetic Voting Map, which consolidates the voting instructions submitted remotely by its shareholders and received by the Central Depository (B3 S.A. – Brasil, Bolsa, Balcão), the share registrar (Banco Bradesco S.A.), and the Company, with respect items 2 and 3 included in the call notice for the Extraordinary General Meeting (“Meeting”), as validated, to be held on July 22, 2026.

 

The information set forth in the Synthetic Voting Map is available in the attached table and corresponds to votes representing 2,066,031,747 shares for the Meeting, equivalent to 48.5% of the Company’s voting capital¹. The Company clarifies that the map disclosed today does not include the votes of holders of American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), which will be represented at the Meeting by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., in its capacity as depositary bank.
